We're a healthcare-technology consulting and engineering firm. We help health systems, payers, EHR vendors, and health-data companies move real clinical data and put AI to work inside the systems they already run, without the compliance exposure of standing up one more silo.

### What we build

- **FHIR &amp; interoperability** — HL7v2 → FHIR, TEFCA / QHIN, USCDI, bulk FHIR, and the data pipelines that make systems actually talk to each other.
- **AI on the record** — ambient and clinical AI, documentation, and workflow automation that runs against your EHR, not a copy of it.
- **Strategy &amp; advisory** — interoperability and AI roadmaps for teams that know what they need and want it shipped.

### Who we work with

**Infirmary Health** · **HealthVerity** · **1upHealth** · **Braided** — and other teams building the data and AI layer of modern healthcare.

### How we work

- **Privacy-first.** PHI stays where it belongs. We de-identify at the edge and never route patient data through tools that shouldn't see it.
- **Engineering-forward.** Senior builders, not slideware. We ship working software.
- **On your stack.** Epic, Oracle Health (Cerner), athenahealth, eClinicalWorks — we meet the EHR where it is.
