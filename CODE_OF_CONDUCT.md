# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want every interaction across 1Putt Health's projects — issues, pull requests, discussions — to be professional, respectful, and welcoming to people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and be patient with questions.
- Respect confidentiality — never share client information, credentials, or protected health information (PHI).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to [conduct@1putthealth.com](mailto:conduct@1putthealth.com) or [hello@1putthealth.com](mailto:hello@1putthealth.com). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may ban, temporarily or permanently, anyone whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

This code applies within all project spaces and when representing the project in public.
