# Security Policy

Security is core to how we work — we build for healthcare, where the cost of getting it wrong is real. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will work with you in good faith.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Report privately, one of two ways:

1. **GitHub private advisory** — on the affected repository, go to **Security → Report a vulnerability** (GitHub private vulnerability reporting).
2. **Email** — [security@1putthealth.com](mailto:security@1putthealth.com) (or [hello@1putthealth.com](mailto:hello@1putthealth.com)).

Please include:

- what you found and where (repo, URL, or component),
- steps to reproduce or a proof of concept,
- the impact you think it has.

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement** within **3 business days**.
- An assessment and a remediation plan as soon as we've confirmed it.
- Credit for your finding if you'd like it, once a fix has shipped.

## Scope &amp; safe harbor

Test only against systems and accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate other people's data, degrade service, or run automated scans that could affect availability. Do not access, store, or share any protected health information (PHI) you may encounter — stop and report it instead. Research conducted in good faith and consistent with this policy will not be pursued or reported by us.
